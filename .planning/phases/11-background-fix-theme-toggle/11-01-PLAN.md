<objective>
Turn `theme.py` from a single flat light-only palette into a dual-tokenized
source of truth (LIGHT + DARK dicts plus a `tokens(mode)` lookup), using the
exact bespoke dark hex values and measured contrast ratios locked in
`11-UI-SPEC.md`.

Purpose: every downstream consumer (state.py Plotly figures, app.py component
props, the html/body background) resolves colors from one place. Without this,
each consumer would invent its own dark hex literals — the exact discipline
breach `11-CONTEXT.md` forbids.

Output: dual-token `theme.py`, updated `test_theme.py`, new `test_theme_tokens.py`.
</objective>
