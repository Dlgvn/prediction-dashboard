## STOP — 03-RESEARCH.md Pattern 2 implements the WRONG MODEL. Do not use it.

Pattern 2 shows an iterative `VAR(lag=1)` fit on percent changes with cumprod level
reconstruction. That is Phase 2's `_VARIterativeWrapper`, which is the RUNNER-UP and was
explicitly rejected. STATE.md records the reason: "iterative VAR family shows explosive
MAPE at long horizons ... direct-OLS VAR variant stays bounded."

The binding winner in `02-MODEL-DECISIONS.md` is:
`Direct-OLS VAR-system(h=1..12) system=[ppan, hdan, baltic_an, urals]`,
`horizon_strategy: direct`, mean 1-12 MAPE 23.80%.

The planner verified its actual implementation in `backend_research/run_var_vecm_wf.py`
lines 220-235 (which delegates to `run_arima_sarimax_wf._DirectMultistepWrapper`,
lines 97-134). It is NOT a `statsmodels.tsa.api.VAR` object at all. It is:

- Twelve independent `sm.OLS` regressions, one per horizon h in 1..12.
- Feature columns, in this exact order: `hdan`, `baltic_an`, `urals` (system members, at
  PRICE LEVELS, not percent changes), then `ppan_lag1`, `ppan_lag2`, `ppan_lag3`
  (the target's own lags 1-3).
- For model h: the response is `ppan.shift(-h)`, regressed on those features with
  `sm.add_constant`, after a joint `dropna()`.
- Prediction: take the SINGLE last available feature row and push it through all twelve
  fitted models. There is no iteration and no feeding of predictions back as inputs.
- If a given h has fewer than 10 usable rows after dropna, that model is skipped and its
  prediction is NaN.

Two important consequences:
1. **No future exog and no predictor sub-models are needed for PPAN.** The direct-OLS
   design uses only features observable today. Do not build a future-exog frame here.
2. **D-03 is satisfied structurally, not by discarding anything.** This model produces no
   HDAN forecast whatsoever — `hdan` enters only as a right-hand-side feature at its last
   observed level. Record this in the SUMMARY: D-03's "never surface the VAR-internal HDAN
   forecast" is trivially satisfied because no such forecast exists in the winning variant.
   `forecast_hdan`'s SARIMAX output remains the sole HDAN authority.

Correspondingly, 03-RESEARCH.md Assumption A1 and Open Question 2 ("verify the VAR lag from
wf_var_vecm.json") are MOOT. There is no VAR lag in this model. Do not spend time on them.
</planner_correction>
